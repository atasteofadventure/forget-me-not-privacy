Your garden, with optional backup

Forget Me Not stores your name, friends’ names, birthdays, check-in history, notes, linked contact details, preferences and garden progress on your device. You can use the garden without an account. If you choose an account, the app also backs up your garden as described below. We do not add advertising or analytics, or sell your information. Your operating system may separately include app data in device backups.

Accounts and cloud backups

You can create an account or sign in with Apple or Google. The provider shares an account identifier, email address and any basic profile information included in its sign-in response with Supabase, our authentication and database provider. Apple may supply a private relay email if you choose Hide My Email. We use this identity to manage your account; the app does not receive your Apple or Google password. A sign-in session is stored on this device. While signed in, the app sends your name, friends’ names and birthdays, check-in intervals and history, saved notes and note follow-up dates, plant choices, garden positions, decorations and unlock progress to your private account backup. Linked contact identifiers and phone numbers, unfinished note drafts, device permissions and preferences are excluded. Supabase and your sign-in provider also process technical connection and authentication information needed to operate and protect the service. Backups are access-controlled to your account; they are not end-to-end encrypted. We use this information to provide account access and garden recovery.

Recovering your garden

Backups run when the app is open and connected, after garden changes and when you return to the app. Settings → Garden backup shows whether saving has finished. On another phone, choose the same Apple or Google sign-in option and account, then choose Add my saved garden. Recovery adds the most recently saved garden to this phone and keeps its existing friends. Each installation keeps a separate backup; changes do not automatically merge between phones. Relink contacts and enable reminders on the new phone. Keep your old garden until you have checked the restored copy.

Skipping and logging out

Account creation is optional after your first three friends. If you skip it, the app offers it once more after watering on or after your fifth distinct day of activity, counted from this feature’s installation. You can also open Keep my garden safe in Settings. Log out leaves this phone’s garden and the saved cloud backup in place, and pauses cloud backups until you sign in again.

Optional contact linking

Choose Link in a friend’s pane to select one contact and a phone number. The app saves that contact’s device identifier, display name, chosen number and number label locally. If the contact has a birthday, its month and day are saved as the friend’s birthday and used for reminders. On iPhone, the contact picker does not require address-book permission; Android asks for Contacts access. The app does not import your address book or modify device contacts. You can change or unlink the selection in the friend’s pane. The saved number is a snapshot; choose the contact again if their number changes.

Optional dictation

If you choose to dictate a note, the app asks for microphone and speech-recognition access. Your device’s speech-recognition service may send audio to Apple or your device’s speech provider for processing. The app saves the resulting text as a note; it does not save audio recordings. Typing notes does not require these permissions. You can revoke access in device settings.

Reminders and your lock screen

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They can show a friend’s name and, for a note follow-up, your note’s text in notifications. Use your device’s notification preview settings to control what appears on the lock screen. You can turn reminders off in the app’s Settings.

Opening a linked friend’s reminder

Tapping a notification for a linked friend opens your messaging app addressed to the saved number and records a watering once Messages opens. Watering and birthday reminders prefill an editable suggested message; private note text is not copied into the draft. You choose whether to send a message. The app cannot confirm whether a message was sent, so that watering remains even if you cancel the message. If Messages cannot open, no watering is recorded. Without a linked number, the notification opens the friend’s pane.

Changing and deleting data

You can edit a friend’s rhythm and birthday, unlink contacts, delete individual notes, and remove a friend by dragging their plant to the removal area. Removing a friend also deletes their notes, linked contact and check-in history; the next successful backup updates this installation’s saved copy. While signed in, Settings → Delete my account and garden deletes your account, all its cloud garden backups and this phone’s garden. If cloud deletion fails, the app keeps this phone’s garden for retry. While logged out, Delete my garden only erases this phone’s data; sign in first to delete an existing account and its cloud backups. Other phones may retain local copies, and device contacts are not deleted. Cloud backups are retained until account deletion; provider recovery backups and security records may remain under the provider’s retention processes. Previously created device backups are controlled by your backup provider.

Contacting support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information and attachments you choose to include. We use them to respond and troubleshoot, and retain the conversation as long as needed for your request and related legal obligations. Email providers handle the conversation. You can ask us about access, correction or deletion of account data and support correspondence. We may need to verify account ownership; information legally required to be retained may be kept. We cannot recover a garden that was only stored on a lost device and was never successfully backed up.

Opening website links

The online privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive your IP address and request details when you visit, under its privacy statement. We have not added analytics, advertising or tracking to that page. Other linked websites apply their own privacy policies. Your garden and notes are not attached to those links.

Read the Supabase privacy policy, Apple privacy policy, Google privacy policy, and GitHub Privacy Statement for those providers’ practices.

Reminders aren't arriving

Check that Notifications is on in the app and allowed for Forget Me Not in your iPhone's Settings. Focus modes and notification settings can affect when reminders appear.

A note or garden change won't save

Keep the app open, check that your device has available storage, and try again. If the error continues, contact support with the error message and your app and iOS versions.

Sign-in and backup errors

For a backup error, reconnect and open Settings → Garden backup → Try backup again. Check the last successful backup before replacing or resetting your phone. If sign-in fails, check your connection and retry Apple or Google. To recover an existing garden, use the same sign-in option and account as before, including the same Apple account if you used Hide My Email. Canceling sign-in keeps the garden on this phone. Contact support if you still cannot access your backup.
