Your garden stays on your device

Forget Me Not is a friendship reminder and journaling app. This policy covers the current iOS release and this privacy and support website.

The app has no accounts, cloud sync, advertising, or analytics. Your garden data is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us. Optional dictation, device backups, support email, and visits to this website are explained below.

Information the app stores

The app saves information you enter and information needed to make your garden work:

  • Your name and the names and birthdays you enter for friends.
  • For contacts you choose to link: their device contact identifier, display name, chosen phone number and number label. If available, their birthday's month and day are saved as the friend's birthday for reminders.
  • Friendship check-in intervals, check-in dates and history, notes, and note follow-up dates.
  • Garden progress, plant choices and positions, and unlocked plants.
  • Your settings, reminder choices and scheduling history, and which friendship facts you have seen.

This information is used on your device to display your garden, keep your notes, calculate progress, schedule reminders and open a text to a linked friend. The app does not access your location. We do not sell your information or share your garden data with advertising or analytics services.

Optional contact linking

Choose Link in a friend's pane to select one contact and a phone number. The iPhone contact picker does not require permission to read your address book; Android requires Contacts permission. The app keeps only the selected contact details listed above on your device. It does not import your address book or change your device contacts. You can change or unlink the selection in the friend's pane. The saved number is a snapshot; choose the contact again if their number changes.

Tapping a notification for a linked friend opens your messaging app with the saved number as the recipient and records a watering once Messages opens. You decide whether to send a message. The app cannot confirm sending, so the watering remains even if you cancel the message. If Messages cannot open, no watering is recorded. Without a linked number, a notification opens the friend's pane. Your messaging provider handles any message you choose to send.

Optional voice dictation

If you choose to dictate a note, the app requests microphone and speech-recognition permission. Apple's speech-recognition service processes your speech and may send audio and related recognition data to Apple, depending on the device, language, and system settings. Apple's handling of this information is covered by its privacy policy.

Forget Me Not saves the resulting text when you save the note. The app does not save audio recordings or send recordings to us. You can type notes without granting either permission, and you can revoke permissions in your iPhone's Settings.

Reminders and notification previews

Reminders are scheduled locally through your device's notification system. They may display a friend's name, a birthday, or the text of a note follow-up, including on your lock screen. You control notification permission and previews in your iPhone's Settings. You can also turn reminders off in the app.

Device storage and backups

Your garden remains stored on your device until you delete it. The app relies on the operating system's app storage protections. Your device or backup provider may include app data in iCloud or computer backups, depending on your settings. These backups are managed by you and your backup provider; they are separate from the app and are not a Forget Me Not cloud-sync service.

We cannot view or recover your locally stored garden. Protect your device and review your backup and notification settings if you keep sensitive notes.

Your choices and deleting data

  • You can change a friend's reminder interval and birthday, and delete individual notes.
  • You can unlink a contact from a friend's pane. Removing a friend's plant also deletes that friend's linked contact, notes and check-in history from the app. Your device contacts are not deleted.
  • Settings → Delete my garden erases the garden, profile, notes, history, and preferences stored by this app on this device, and requests removal of its pending and delivered notifications.
  • You can turn off reminders in the app and revoke notification, microphone, or speech-recognition permission in device Settings.

If notification cleanup fails, the app reports it; you can also clear notifications or turn them off in device Settings. Deleting data in the app does not erase previously created device backups. Manage those through your backup provider. There is no account to delete in this release.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information and attachments you choose to include. We use them to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Email is handled by the email providers involved in delivering and storing the conversation.

Please share only what is needed to explain the problem. We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to address your request and any related legal obligations. You can ask us to delete it using the contact below; information we are legally required to retain may be kept. We cannot delete garden data remotely because we do not receive it.

This website and external links

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address and request details when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We have not added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies to this page.

If you open an external link from the app or this page, the destination website handles your visit under its own privacy policy. Your garden and notes are not attached to those links.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our privacy practices change. If a future release introduces accounts, cloud services, or new data uses, we will explain those changes and request any required permissions before you use those features.

Get support

For help with Forget Me Not or a privacy request, contact Forget Me Not support at sarah@atasteofadventure.com.

Reminders aren't arriving

Check that Notifications is on in the app and allowed for Forget Me Not in your iPhone's Settings. Focus modes and notification settings can affect when reminders appear.

A note or garden change won't save

Keep the app open, check that your device has available storage, and try again. If the error continues, contact support with the error message and your app and iOS versions.

Moving to a new phone

This release has no account-based sync or recovery. Whether your garden transfers depends on your device backup and migration settings. Avoid deleting your original data until you have checked the new device.
